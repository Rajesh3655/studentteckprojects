Privacy Policy

Transparency regarding how we handle your data and cookies.

Last Updated: March 24, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you visit Student Tech Project Hub, we may automatically collect non-personally identifiable information such as your IP address, browser type, referring website, operating system, and pages visited. If you use our contact form, we collect the name and email address you voluntarily provide solely to respond to your inquiry.

2. Use of Cookies

We use cookies to improve your experience on our site, analyze our traffic, and serve personalized advertisements. A cookie is a small text file stored on your device that helps us understand how you interact with our platform.

3. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to monitor site traffic and user behavior. Google Analytics collects data such as how often users visit the site, what pages they visit, and what other sites they used prior to coming to ours. We use this information solely to improve our website. Google's ability to use and share information collected by Google Analytics is restricted by the Google Analytics Terms of Use and the Google Privacy Policy.

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on our website. As a third-party vendor, Google uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website and other websites on the internet.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of some third-party vendors' uses of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

5. Third-Party Links

Our website contains links to other academic and research websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or the content of such third-party sites. We encourage you to review their respective privacy policies when visiting them.

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at hello@studenttechprojects.in.
